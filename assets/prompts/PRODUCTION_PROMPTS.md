# Production prompts
Attach the class board and accepted character model to each generation request.
Do not generate every character independently without references.

## Warrior model revision
Preserve the supplied warrior identity: chestnut hair, steel armor, brown leather,
burgundy scarf/cape, right-hand sword, left-hand shield. Create one complete game-ready
idle pose facing the requested direction with a slightly elevated three-quarter view.
Detailed stylized pixel art with coherent pixel clusters. Genuine transparent background,
no glow or translucent color wash outside the silhouette, no shadow, text or border.
All weapons and boots visible. Review at 100–130px actor height before approval.

## Action frames
Using the accepted model, create [ACTION] as [FRAME COUNT] sequential poses facing
[DIRECTION]. Preserve face, armor, weapon hands, camera, palette and body scale.
Describe the motion explicitly: [anticipation/contact/recovery or walk contacts/passing].
Keep the actor aligned to a stable ground pivot, with room for weapons.
No text, grids, backgrounds or baked shadows. These are real distinct poses, not
translated copies. Review frames individually and in sequence before building a sheet.
Generating a sheet does not guarantee exact cell alignment; measure and correct it.

## Bat
Original shadow bat, dark plum wing membranes, charcoal fur, small amber eyes,
clear readable ears and wing silhouette. Match the accepted warrior's detail level,
camera and upper-left light. Transparent background, no baked shadow.
For flight use distinct upstroke, transition, downstroke and recovery poses.

## Archer concept proposal
Original dark-fantasy chibi archer, moss cloak, leather outfit, readable bow,
ochre accents, about three heads tall. Match the attached class board's visual language.
Create a model concept, not an animation sheet. Approval pending.

## Environment overlays
Create an isolated [fog/foreground branch/lantern/smoke] layer matching the supplied
environment camera and light. Preserve a quiet combat area. Transparent background.
Do not redraw the entire scene when only an overlay is requested.
